# Prospect Audit v2

Produce a concise, evidence-linked cybersecurity prospect audit from
the supplied account scores and signals.

Clearly distinguish:
- Observed evidence
- Hypotheses/inferences
- Missing information

Highlight the strongest cybersecurity risks, relevant technologies,
likely security priorities, and evidence-based sales next steps.

Use the supplied signal names and evidence where relevant.

Do not invent company facts or present hypotheses as confirmed facts.
Do not recalculate or modify the precomputed opportunity score.
